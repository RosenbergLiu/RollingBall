# Free Fall Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development (recommended) or superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** The ball can land on any platform below it; Game Over only below the baseline; gates count only when passed through; no progress bar; wind while airborne.

**Architecture:** The path tracker re-finds the surface under the ball (all pieces, any height) whenever it is off its spot or airborne. Finish and checkpoints become gate-crossing tests on the ball's move from the previous to the current physics step (`src/game/gates.ts`). Falls are a plain height test against `level.baseline`. Wind is a new looped noise layer in `SoundGraph`, mixed by pure rules like the rolling sound.

**Tech Stack:** TypeScript, three.js, Rapier, Web Audio, Vitest.

**Spec:** `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-24-free-fall-design.md`

## Global Constraints

- Baseline: 3 m under the level's lowest platform surface (`BASELINE_DEPTH = 3`).
- Gate opening: between the posts (|u| < post offset) and from 0.5 m under the gate's surface point up to the bottom of the bar / banner. Forward crossings only.
- Landing sound after ≥ 0.25 s in the air. Wind fades out over ~0.5 s after Game Over.
- Sounds stay synthesized (no audio files) and under the existing limiter.

## Review Focus

- Ball rolling normally on an upper spiral loop must never jump to the loop below (tracker window still applies while grounded).
- Ball in the air over two stacked platforms must track the *upper* one (closest below), not the one it was over first.
- Respawning on a checkpoint gate must not re-trigger it, and an uphill gate the respawned ball rolls through again changes nothing.
- A ball flying high over the finish banner, or landing on the run-out past the finish, must not win.
- Every existing level must still be finished by the autopilot (gates crossed on the centerline).
